Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password, along with the expense records you log — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to operate your account, display your spending dashboard and reports, and improve the overall functionality of Spendly. We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal financial data with third parties. We may rely on trusted infrastructure providers to host the service, who are bound to handle data securely and only as needed to operate Spendly.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website.
